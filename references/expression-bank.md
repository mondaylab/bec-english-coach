# Expression Bank

Use these as flexible patterns, not scripts to memorize.

## Opening A Discussion

- To begin with, what do you think about...?
- First, let's look at...
- Shall we start with...?
- How would you approach this?

## Giving An Opinion

- In my view, the most important factor is...
- I would say that... because...
- From a business perspective, ...
- The main benefit is...

## Agreeing

- That's a good point.
- I completely agree.
- That's exactly how I see it.
- I agree, and I would add that...

## Polite Disagreement

- I see your point, but my concern is...
- That's fair, but I'm not sure it would work in this case.
- I understand the argument, but we also need to consider...
- That's not quite what I had in mind.

## Adding Evidence

- Research suggests that...
- From my experience, ...
- One example would be...
- This could lead to...
- The risk is that...

## Summarizing

- So, to sum up, we think the best option is...
- It seems we agree that...
- Overall, our priority should be...
- Shall we make that our final recommendation?

## Follow-Up And Workplace Communication

- I just wanted to check in on...
- Could you share an update on...?
- Would it be possible to...?
- Let me know if you need anything from my side.
- Thanks for the clarification.

## Email Tone Upgrades

Less natural:

```text
Please finish it quickly.
```

Better:

```text
Would it be possible to send this over by the end of the day?
```

Less natural:

```text
I want to know your progress.
```

Better:

```text
I just wanted to check in and see how things are progressing.
```
