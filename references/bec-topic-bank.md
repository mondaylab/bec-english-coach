# BEC Topic Bank

Use these themes to select daily prompts. Rotate topics instead of drilling the same domain repeatedly.

## Core Themes

- Company operations and management
- Human resource management
- Marketing
- Business travel
- Corporate public relations
- Company development
- E-commerce and new ways of working
- Business meetings

## Starter Prompts

### Company Operations and Management

- What qualities should a good team leader have?
- How can a company improve internal communication?
- What should a company consider when trying to reduce costs?
- How can employees deal with a heavy workload?

### Human Resource Management

- What should companies consider when recruiting new staff?
- How can a company motivate employees?
- Why is employee training important?
- What can managers do to reduce staff turnover?

### Marketing

- What makes a marketing campaign successful?
- How can a company understand customer needs?
- What should a business consider before launching a new product?

### Business Travel

- What are the advantages and disadvantages of business travel?
- How can companies control travel costs?
- What should employees prepare before visiting a client?

### Corporate Public Relations

- How should a company handle negative customer feedback?
- What can businesses do to build a good public image?
- Why is transparency important in corporate communication?

### Company Development

- What should a company consider before expanding into a new market?
- How can a company encourage innovation?
- What risks may appear during rapid growth?

### E-Commerce and New Ways of Working

- What are the benefits of remote work?
- How can companies keep online teams productive?
- What should businesses consider when selling products online?

### Business Meetings

- What makes a meeting effective?
- How can a chairperson encourage participation?
- What should be included in meeting follow-up?

## Part 3 Roleplay Pattern

Use this format:

```text
The company you work for is considering [business decision].
Discuss the situation together and decide:
- what factors should be considered
- what action the company should take
```

For daily practice, choose one theme, one decision, and two decision points.
