# Business English Coach

Business English Coach is a lightweight skill for BEC speaking practice and workplace English coaching. It is designed for short daily sessions, especially a 20-minute morning routine that starts with model answers and scaffolds in early sessions, then gradually moves toward independent production.

## What It Helps With

- 20-minute daily Business English practice
- BEC Speaking Part 1, Part 2, and Part 3 drills
- Workplace English for meetings, emails, clients, and follow-ups
- Chinglish correction and natural business expression upgrades
- Daily learning cards and personal error-pattern tracking

## Default Training Order

1. Part 2 mini-presentation: build structured speaking first
2. Part 1 interview: improve short, natural answers
3. Part 3 collaborative task: practice interaction, negotiation, and summarizing

## 72-Day Rotation

- Days 1-24: Level 1, show a model answer first; the learner imitates and adapts.
- Days 25-48: Level 2, show direction and sentence starters first; show the model answer after the first attempt.
- Days 49-72: Level 3, exam-style practice; show only the prompt first.

Each 24-day round follows `Part 2 → Part 1 → Part 3`. Within each part, rotate through eight topics: company operations and management, human resource management, marketing, business travel, corporate public relations, company development, e-commerce and new ways of working, and business meetings.

## Daily 20-Minute Flow

1. 2 minutes: review previous expressions
2. 4 minutes: read the prompt, answer direction, useful expressions, and a short model answer
3. 4 minutes: imitate the model and adapt it with personal details
4. 5 minutes: receive BEC/workplace-focused feedback
5. 3 minutes: upgrade the answer into natural business English and repeat
6. 2 minutes: create a learning card

## Support Levels

- Level 1: show a short model answer first, then ask the learner to imitate and adapt it.
- Level 2: show answer direction, keywords, and sentence starters first; show the model after the first attempt.
- Level 3: show only the prompt first, then provide feedback and a model answer afterward.

## Example Prompts

```text
Start today's Business English practice.
Run a 20-minute BEC speaking session.
Simulate BEC Speaking Part 3 with me.
Use Level 1 today and show me a model answer first.
Make this sentence sound more professional.
Turn today's practice into a learning card.
```
