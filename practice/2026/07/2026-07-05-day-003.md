# Day 003 - BEC Part 2 - 市场营销

日期：2026-07-05
主题：市场营销
题目：What should a business consider before launching a new product?
辅助等级：Level 1
训练重点：解释收益和风险

## 我的原始回答

Before launching a new product, a business should consider several important factors. First, customer needs are important because the product should solve a real problem for customers. Another key factor is market competition, especially if there are already similar products available. For example, if the company does not understand its competitors, it may set the wrong price and fail to attract target customers. Overall, the company should make sure the product is useful, competitive, and clearly positioned.
