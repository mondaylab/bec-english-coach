# Day 004 - BEC Part 2 - 商务旅行

日期：2026-08-01
主题：商务旅行
题目：What should employees prepare before visiting a client?
辅助等级：Level 1
训练重点：对比优势和准备事项

## 我的原始回答

Before visiting a client, employees should prepare several important things.

First, understanding the client's background is important because it helps the communicate more effectively.

Another key preparation is to clarify the meeting objectives, especially if they need to discuss a proposal or solve a problem.

For example, if employees know what the client expects, they can prepare better questions and relevant materials in advance.

Overall, good preparation can help the meeting run smoothly and create a professional impression.

## 修改后的商务英语
