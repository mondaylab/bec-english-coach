# Day 005 - BEC Part 2 - 企业公关

日期：2026-08-02
主题：企业公关
题目：How should a company handle negative customer feedback?
辅助等级：Level 1
训练重点：解释信任、形象和透明度

## 我的原始回答

When handling negative customer feedback, a company should take several important steps.

First, the company should respond to customers seriously and politely because this shows that it values its opinions.

Another key step is to identify the real problem and offer a practical solution, especially if the feedback is about product quality or service.

For example, if many customers complain that deliveries are late, the company should find out why and tell customers how it will solve the problem.

Overall, handling feedback well can help the company protect its reputation and build customer trust.
