## 修改后的商务英语

When it comes to being a good team leader, there are several qualities to consider. First, clear communication is very important because it helps the team understand their tasks and priorities. Another key quality is the ability to motivate people, especially when the team is under pressure. For example, when the team is working on a difficult project, a good leader can encourage team members and help them stay focused. Overall, a good team leader should help the team work efficiently and achieve better results.

## 今日表达

- clear communication
- the ability to motivate people
- stay focused
- work efficiently
- achieve better results

## 今日错误

- it effort the team -> it helps the team / it affects the team
- motivate people -> the ability to motivate people
- achieve their results -> achieve better results / achieve its goals
