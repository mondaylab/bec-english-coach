## 修改后的商务英语

When recruiting new staff, companies should consider several important factors. First, relevant experience is important because experienced candidates can adapt to the role more quickly. Another key factor is communication skills, especially if the role involves teamwork or client contact. For example, a candidate who can explain ideas clearly may help the team avoid misunderstandings and work more efficiently. Overall, companies should choose people who can do the job well and work smoothly with others.
