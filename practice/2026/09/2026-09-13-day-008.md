# Day 008 - BEC Part 2 - 商务会议

日期：2026-09-13
主题：商务会议
题目：What makes a meeting effective?
辅助等级：Level 1
训练重点：解释高效会议的关键，并把讨论转化为明确行动

## 我的原始回答

When organizing a meeting, there are several factors to consider.

First, a clear agenda is important because it helps participants stay focused.

Another key factor is active participation, as different perspectives can lead to better decisions.

For example, the chairperson can invite quiter team members to share their views.

Overall, an effective meeting should have a clear purpose and produce specific action points.

## 修改后的商务英语

When organizing a meeting, there are several factors to consider.

First, a clear agenda is important because it helps participants stay focused.

Another key factor is active participation, as different perspectives can lead to better decisions.

For example, the chairperson can invite quieter team members to share their views.

Overall, an effective meeting should have a clear purpose and produce specific action points.

## Business strategy extension

对标对象：AI 产品团队的每周跨部门会议

商业场景：团队需要在会议中形成清晰决策，并把任务分配给具体负责人，设置明确的完成时间。

核心判断：高效会议应当推动决策和执行。明确负责人、任务和截止时间，可以减少会后理解偏差，让讨论真正产生业务结果。

中文解释：会议效率不只来自议程和参与度，也来自清晰的会后跟进。每项任务都需要负责人和截止时间。

英文策略表达：

An effective meeting should lead to clear decisions and actions.

First, the team should assign a clear owner to each task.

It should also set specific deadlines so that everyone knows what to do next.

Overall, clear follow-up helps the team turn discussion into results.

可复用句型：

- An effective meeting should lead to clear decisions and actions.
- The team should assign a clear owner to each task.
- Set specific deadlines so that everyone knows what to do next.
- Clear follow-up helps the team turn discussion into results.
- Turn discussion into results.

反馈重点：`discuss` 是动词，`discussion` 是名词；`turn A into B` 中 A 和 B 通常使用名词。
